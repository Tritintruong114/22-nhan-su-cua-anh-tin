# Initial template catalog

## editorial-hero
Single image. Large headline with visual subject on the right and negative space on the left.

## bold-statement
Single statement. Minimal composition, high contrast, strong brand mark.

## framework
Portrait card or carousel. Numbered principles with one idea per block.

## before-after
Two-column or split-screen comparison. Use only claims supported by the brief.

## diagram-carousel
Portrait carousel. One system/process per slide, rendered with SVG for exact labels.

## screenshot-story
Product screenshot with deterministic callouts and a short narrative.

## personal-brand-ai-visual
User portrait or approved reference image combined with an editorial AI-generated scene.
