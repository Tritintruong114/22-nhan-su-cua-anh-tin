How to Setup Your First ClawExperts Agent in 2 Minutes
A step-by-step guide to configuring your account and deploying a working AI agent without any code.
Table of Contents
1 Step 1 - Pick a plan
2 Step 2 - Your agent spins up automatically
3 Step 3 - Connect your messaging channel
4 Step 4 - Start using it
Setting up an AI agent yourself sounds simple - until you're three hours deep into Docker errors, Python version conflicts, and port collisions you didn't know existed. Most people give up before they ever get to use the thing.
ClawExperts fixes that. You get a fully managed, always-on AI agent without touching a single config file.
Here's how to get yours running in under two minutes.
Step 1 - Pick a plan
Go to clawexperts.com and choose the plan that fits your needs. If you're not sure, start with the entry-level tier - you can upgrade anytime. Every plan comes with a dedicated private instance, daily backups, and zero maintenance on your end.
Step 2 - Your agent spins up automatically
Right after checkout, ClawExperts provisions a dedicated instance configured specifically for you. No setup wizard, no waiting on support. Your agent is live within minutes.
Step 3 - Connect your messaging channel
This is where it gets real. Connect your agent to Telegram, Discord, or any supported channel so you can talk to it naturally - from your phone, your desktop, wherever you are.
For Telegram (the easiest option):
Open Telegram and search for @BotFather
Type /newbot and follow the prompts to name your bot
BotFather sends you a bot token - copy the whole string
Open your ClawExperts dashboard, paste the token in, and say: "This is my Telegram bot token: [token], please help me configure the Telegram connection."
Done. Your agent starts receiving messages immediately.
Step 4 - Start using it
Open your connected channel and just talk to it. Ask it to draft an email, summarize a document, review code, manage your calendar, or automate something you've been doing manually for months. Your ClawExperts agent remembers context across conversations and gets things done while you focus elsewhere.
The hardest part of having an AI agent used to be getting it running. Now that part takes two minutes. Everything after that is just you deciding what to hand off first.
