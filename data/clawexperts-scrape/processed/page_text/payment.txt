Payment Policy
1. PAYMENT METHODS
Customers can top up credits or subscribe to plans on ClawExperts using:
International cards: Visa, MasterCard, and other cards supported by Stripe
E-wallets & banking apps: Available methods supported by Stripe (depending on region)
Promo codes: Discounts or trial credits issued during campaigns
2. PAYMENT DETAILS
Cards & International Payments
We accept Visa and MasterCard issued by domestic and international banks
Transactions are processed securely via Stripe
Customers must ensure sufficient balance and complete required authentication (e.g. 3D Secure)
Stripe Payment Gateway
All payments are encrypted and processed by Stripe
ClawExperts does not store card information
E-Invoice
A payment confirmation and e-invoice will be sent to your registered email after successful payment
3. ACCEPTED CARDS
We support most international cards via Stripe, including:
Visa
MasterCard
American Express (Amex)
JCB
Discover & Diners Club
China UnionPay (CUP)
4. FAILED TRANSACTIONS
A payment may fail due to:
Authentication failure: Incorrect OTP or 3D Secure verification
Insufficient funds or limits exceeded
Incorrect card details: Card number, expiry date, or CVC/CVV
International payments not enabled on the card
Note:
If a transaction fails but funds were deducted, please contact:
Email:
team@clawexperts.com
Or your issuing bank for support
