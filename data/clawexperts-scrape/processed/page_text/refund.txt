Refund Policy
Last updated: 01.04.2026
This is a summary of ClawExperts’ refund policy.
In case of any conflict, the Terms of Service shall prevail.
1. BETA PHASE
During the Beta phase (from 01.04.2026), we offer a simple and transparent refund policy:
Request a refund within 30 days of payment
No reason required
Applies to all paid users during the Beta phase
Access will be terminated within 24 hours after refund
This policy is designed to let you try the product risk-free.
2. POST-BETA PHASE
The unconditional refund policy will no longer apply.
Cancellations and refunds will follow the rules below.
3. MONTHLY SUBSCRIPTION
Cancel renewal anytime
No cancellation fees
Effect:
Access continues until the end of the current billing cycle
No charges for subsequent cycles after cancellation
4. ANNUAL SUBSCRIPTION
Cancel anytime
The current month remains active
Refund calculation:
Used months are charged at the standard monthly rate
The remaining balance will be refunded
Example:
You paid 1,188,000 VND/year (Lite Plan) and cancel after 4 months:
Usage fee: 4 × 99,000 = 396,000 VND
Refund: 792,000 VND
5. REFUND LIMITS
We reserve the right to refuse refunds in cases of:
Policy abuse
Multiple accounts created to exploit refunds
Violation of Terms of Use
6. REFUND REQUEST
Submit your request via:
Email:
team@clawexperts.com
Include:
Registered account email
Refund request
Response time: 1–2 business days
7. REFUND PROCESSING
Refunds are issued via the original payment method
Processing time: 5–10 business days
Actual receipt time depends on your bank or payment provider
All refunds are calculated in VND

This policy ensures a fair experience for all users
