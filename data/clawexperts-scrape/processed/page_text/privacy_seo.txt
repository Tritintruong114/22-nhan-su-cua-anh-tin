Privacy Policy — SEO Agent
A supplement to the general ClawExperts Privacy Policy.
Last updated: 14 July 2026
Applies to: the SEO Agent (ClawExpert) module on the ClawExperts platform.
0. SCOPE OF THIS DOCUMENT
This supplement adds to, and does not replace, the general ClawExperts Privacy Policy (available at
https://clawexperts.com/privacy
). The general policy still governs your account, billing, system logs, and other general data.
This document describes specifically how SEO Agent handles data when you connect external services: Google Search Console (GSC) and WordPress . Connecting these services is optional and can be done later; SEO Agent accesses them only when you actively grant permission.
1. GOOGLE SEARCH CONSOLE (GSC) CONNECTION
When you grant permission, SEO Agent connects to your Google Search Console account via Google OAuth .
Access scope: read-only ( webmasters.readonly ). SEO Agent does not write, modify, or delete any data in your Search Console account.
Data we read from GSC:
URLs of pages on your website
Search queries (the terms users searched to reach your pages)
Impressions, clicks, and CTR
Average position
Purpose:
display your website's performance on the dashboard
SEO analysis and content planning
performance decay detection over time
2. USE OF DATA RECEIVED FROM GOOGLE APIs (LIMITED USE)
ClawExpert's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the
Google API Services User Data Policy
, including the Limited Use requirements.
Specifically, Google user data (including GSC data):
is used only to provide and improve user-facing features within SEO Agent
is not sold or transferred to third parties for advertising purposes
is not used for advertising of any kind
is not used to train generalized AI models
is not read by humans, except: (a) with your consent for a specific purpose, (b) where necessary for security (e.g. investigating abuse), or (c) where required by law
3. WORDPRESS CONNECTION
When you grant permission, SEO Agent connects to your WordPress website (via OAuth or Application Passwords).
publish blog posts (draft / schedule / publish) to WordPress
read page content to support audits and technical SEO checks
Data involved: connection credentials, blog content you create within SEO Agent, and page data read for analysis. SEO Agent performs write actions (publishing) only after you confirm — it does not auto-publish before approval.
4. HISTORICAL SNAPSHOTS & RETENTION
To detect performance decay honestly, SEO Agent stores periodic snapshots of your GSC performance data (impressions, clicks, and position per URL/query) over time. This is a technical prerequisite: a decline cannot be detected from a single data pull.
Retention period: GSC snapshot data is retained for 13 months , or until you disconnect or request deletion, whichever comes first. Thirteen months is the minimum needed for year-over-year comparison — which distinguishes genuine decay from seasonal fluctuation — and we do not retain it longer than necessary for that purpose.
Our actual system behavior always matches what is stated in this document.
5. DATA SHARING & THIRD PARTIES
We do not sell and do not share your Google user data with third parties for their own purposes. Data is processed only by infrastructure providers necessary to operate the service (sub-processors), and only to that extent:
cloud infrastructure / storage: [SPECIFY: e.g. AWS / GCP]
keyword data service: [SPECIFY: DataForSEO / SEMrush] — note: only seed keywords are sent for lookup; your Google user data is NOT sent
Data may also be shared in response to a valid legal request.
6. DISCONNECTING & REVOKING ACCESS
You may revoke access at any time:
disconnect GSC / WordPress directly in Settings → Connections in SEO Agent, or
revoke the app's access in your Google Account security settings (
https://myaccount.google.com/permissions
)
After disconnecting, SEO Agent stops accessing new data from that service.
7. DATA DELETION
You may request deletion of your data (including stored GSC snapshots) at any time by disconnecting and emailing
team@clawexperts.com
. We will delete the relevant data following the process set out in the general policy.
8. CHANGES
This document may be updated over time; the updated version takes effect upon posting.
