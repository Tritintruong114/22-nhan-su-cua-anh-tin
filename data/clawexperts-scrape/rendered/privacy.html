Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 31, 2026

ClawExperts is committed to protecting your privacy.

This policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your data.

1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

We may collect:

Account information

  • Email address
  • Registration details

Usage data

  • Actions performed on the platform
  • Agent configurations
  • System logs

User-provided data

  • Content, inputs, and data submitted to agents

2. HOW WE USE DATA

We use data to:

  • operate and maintain the service
  • improve system performance
  • provide user support

We do not use your data to train AI models.

3. ISOLATED ENVIRONMENT

Each agent runs in an isolated environment.

This means:

  • no data sharing between users
  • no cross-access between agents

4. DATA SHARING

We do not sell or share your personal data.

Data may only be shared:

  • if required by law
  • with infrastructure providers (e.g. cloud services)

5. SECURITY

We implement reasonable security measures:

  • encrypted data transmission
  • access controls
  • system monitoring

6. DATA RETENTION

Data is stored only as long as necessary to:

  • provide the service
  • comply with legal obligations

You may request data deletion at any time.

7. YOUR RIGHTS

You have the right to:

  • access your data
  • request corrections
  • request deletion

8. COOKIES

We may use cookies to:

  • maintain sessions
  • improve user experience

9. CHANGES

This policy may be updated over time.
