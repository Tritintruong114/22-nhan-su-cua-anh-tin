# B2B Outreach Experiment Email

Use for drafting, reviewing, preparing, or monitoring B2B outreach for ClawExperts / FireGroup enterprise AI Agent work.

## Non-negotiables

- **1 persona → 1 context → 1 workflow hypothesis → 1 outcome → 1 CTA.**
- Outreach is a market-learning experiment, not a generic product announcement.
- Separate verified facts, reasonable inferences, and unknowns. Never turn an inference into a claim about the recipient’s internal operation.
- Never infer a recipient or email address and send. When uncertain, research, propose, and request confirmation.
- Default to **Gmail draft first**. Do not send until the user explicitly approves the exact outbound action.

## Before writing

State compactly:

1. Verified facts relevant to the angle, with source.
2. PIC/persona and likely buyer role in this workflow.
3. Context/trigger hypothesis.
4. Exactly one workflow hypothesis and one outcome to test.
5. Unknowns and adoption risks (control, trust, data access, security, sponsor, timing).
6. Confidence: high / medium / low.
7. Learning signals from reply, non-reply, redirect, or objection.

Minimum account facts: company, domain, named PIC, verified email, approach context (cold/referral/met/follow-up). Missing PIC/email means **do not draft for sending**; research or ask focused questions first.

## Research and copy rules

- Use company facts only where they support: public fact → careful inference → testable hypothesis.
- Do not reintroduce a company to its own employees or stuff an email with research facts.
- Use conditional language: “I wanted to test whether…” rather than “Your team is spending…”
- Do not bundle workflows. One primary workflow; optionally one supporting example.
- Lead with work outcome, not AI Agent features: less searching/aggregation/checking; clearer review items; fewer missed follow-ups; faster decisions.
- Mention controls only if relevant, e.g. a narrow pilot, customer-defined scope/data access, and team review/approval.

## Email sequence

1. Persona + specific-work subject (not product-led).
2. Greeting.
3. One-line FireGroup/OpenClaw introduction.
4. One verified context fact tied directly to the hypothesis.
5. Conditional workflow hypothesis.
6. One concrete customer outcome.
7. Relevant control condition, if needed.
8. 20–30 minute workflow-specific discovery CTA plus “please redirect me if you are not the owner.”
9. Booking link when appropriate: `https://calendar.app.google/BZwoXbtW71TQefHUA`.
10. Approved FireGroup signature from `../../assets/firegroup-email-signature.html`.

## Draft and monitoring record

Before draft creation, record: ICP/persona, trigger, workflow/pain, desired outcome, CTA, evidence, confidence, adoption risks, and review date.

Keep Outreach Step separate from Deal Status:

`Account Research → PIC Identified → First Touch Sent → Follow-up 1 → Follow-up 2 → Replied → Discovery Booked → Discovery Completed → Meeting Proposed → Meeting Booked → No Response / Nurture`

Afterward classify: right/wrong person, pain, or timing; interest but low trust; need but blocked by data/owner/budget; or not ICP. Update the next experiment accordingly.
