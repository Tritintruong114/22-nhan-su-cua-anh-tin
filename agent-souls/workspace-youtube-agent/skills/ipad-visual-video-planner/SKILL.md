# iPad Visual Video Planner

## Purpose

Create a tight YouTube video plan that blends:

- A strong narrative question
- iPad visual drawing as the main thinking surface
- Face-camera moments for trust and emphasis
- Screen demos for proof
- A concrete output by the end

The style should feel practical, fast, and creator-led: the viewer should feel they are watching a system being built in real time, not just hearing a lecture.

## Default Output

When this skill is used, produce a video plan with:

- Core thesis
- Viewer promise
- 10-minute beat structure with timestamps
- iPad visual plan for each section
- Demo/screen recording moments
- Hook options
- Closing line or next-video tease
- Optional title ideas

If the user already has a concept, preserve it and structure around it. Do not over-expand into a full course unless asked.

## Planning Principles

1. Start with the outcome, not background.
   - Open by showing or naming what will exist by the end.
   - Example: “By the end of this video, we will have a ready sales page, a sales agent, a fanpage agent, and a fulfillment flow.”

2. Frame the video around a question with tension.
   - Examples:
     - “Is building a one-person business still hard in the AI era?”
     - “Can one person operate like a small company using AI agents?”
     - “What happens if we stop prompting AI and start hiring digital employees?”

3. Use iPad drawing as the spine.
   - Every 60-90 seconds, add something meaningful to the diagram.
   - The drawing should evolve from confusion into a working system.
   - Use simple boxes, arrows, roles, checklists, and before/after diagrams.

4. Create multiple small peaks.
   - Avoid one long build-up with payoff only at the end.
   - Add a reveal or concrete artifact every 1-2 minutes: diagram, page section, agent role, workflow, checklist, automation message.

5. Make the output real.
   - The video should end with something ready or nearly ready: a page, workflow, agent brief, content plan, template, checklist, or automation flow.
   - Avoid ending with only abstract advice.

6. Keep the scope narrow.
   - For a 10-minute video, choose one transformation.
   - Example: not “how to build a full business”, but “how to turn one product idea into a page plus two digital employees.”

## Recommended 10-Minute Structure

### 0:00-0:30 — Outcome Hook

Show the final state or state it clearly.

Include:
- What will exist by the end
- Why it matters
- A quick visual preview

Good pattern:
“Trong video này, mình sẽ thử dựng một công ty 1 người bằng AI. Cuối video, mình sẽ có một page bán hàng, một nhân sự số tư vấn chốt đơn, một nhân sự số chăm page, và một group báo đơn cho shop giao hàng.”

Visual:
- Quick montage or iPad sketch of the final system
- Do not spend this section on definitions

### 0:30-1:30 — The Question

Introduce the tension.

For AI entrepreneurship:
- “Khởi nghiệp 1 người có còn khó như trước không?”
- “Một người có thể vận hành như một team nhỏ không?”

Visual:
- Draw one person in the center
- Surround them with business tasks: product, page, content, sales, orders, delivery, customer care
- Show the overwhelm first

### 1:30-2:30 — The Reframe

Give the main thesis.

Example:
“Công ty 1 người không có nghĩa là một người làm tất cả. Nó có nghĩa là một người thiết kế hệ thống.”

Visual:
- Replace tasks with roles
- Founder in center
- Digital employees around the founder
- External partner/source-of-goods as part of the system

### 2:30-3:30 — Narrow The Case

Pick the smallest real business case.

Checklist:
- Product/source exists
- Customer can understand it quickly
- Page can sell it
- Conversation can close it
- Fulfillment can be delegated
- Low starting capital or low inventory risk

Visual:
- Draw “product selection filter”
- Cross out ideas that require too much capital, heavy inventory, or complex operations

### 3:30-5:00 — Build The Page

Create or outline the sales page.

The page should contain:
- Product name or offer
- Target customer
- Promise/outcome
- Key benefits
- Proof or reason to believe
- Price or inquiry path
- CTA
- FAQ or objections

Visual:
- iPad wireframe first
- Then screen demo showing the actual page or generated page draft

### 5:00-6:40 — Create The Sales Digital Employee

Frame this as writing a job description, not just prompting.

Sales digital employee should have:
- Role
- Mission
- Inputs
- Conversation flow
- FAQ/objection handling
- Data to collect before order confirmation
- Escalation rules
- Output format for confirmed orders

Visual:
- Employee card:
  - Title: Sales Agent
  - KPI: conversations, qualified leads, confirmed orders
  - Checklist: greet, diagnose, recommend, handle objection, confirm, send order

### 6:40-8:00 — Create The Fanpage Digital Employee

Define the content/page operator.

Fanpage digital employee should handle:
- Post ideas
- Short captions
- Content calendar
- Comment monitoring
- Basic customer engagement
- Reporting what works

Visual:
- Draw traffic loop:
  - Fanpage posts → comments/inbox → sales agent → order

### 8:00-9:00 — Connect Fulfillment

Show the business becoming operational.

For source-of-goods/shop workflows:
- Create a shared group with the shop/source partner
- When sales agent closes an order, it sends structured order details to the group
- Shop confirms and ships
- Agent or founder tracks status and customer follow-up

Visual:
- Draw full flow:
  - Customer sees post
  - Inbox/page
  - Sales agent
  - Confirmed order
  - Group notification
  - Shop delivery
  - Customer care

Order message format example:
```
ĐƠN MỚI
Tên khách:
SĐT:
Địa chỉ:
Sản phẩm:
Số lượng:
Ghi chú:
Trạng thái thanh toán:
```

### 9:00-10:00 — Close With The New Mental Model

Summarize the transformation.

Strong closing pattern:
“Khởi nghiệp trong kỷ nguyên AI không bắt đầu bằng việc thuê thêm người. Nó bắt đầu bằng việc nhìn doanh nghiệp như một hệ thống các vai trò, rồi tạo nhân sự số cho từng vai trò đó.”

End with one of:
- What the viewer can try today
- What the next episode will test
- A clear artifact recap

## iPad Visual Rules

Use simple, legible drawings:
- Boxes for roles
- Arrows for flows
- Checklists for decisions
- Before/after diagrams for reframes
- One diagram should evolve across the video when possible

Avoid:
- Dense text blocks
- Too many arrows at once
- Tiny handwriting
- Decorative visuals that do not advance the explanation

Good recurring visual motif:
- Start with messy task cloud around one person
- Gradually convert the cloud into a clean operating system

## Shot Mix

Recommended mix for a 10-minute video:
- 45-55% iPad drawing
- 25-35% screen demo
- 10-20% face camera

Use face camera for:
- Hook
- Important reframes
- Personal opinion
- Closing

Use screen recording for:
- Generated page
- Agent setup
- Actual workflow proof
- Group/order notification demo

Use iPad for:
- Conceptual clarity
- Transitions
- System diagrams
- Before/after thinking

## Retention Checklist

Before finalizing the plan, check:

- Does the first 30 seconds promise a concrete result?
- Is there a new visual or artifact every 60-90 seconds?
- Is the scope narrow enough for 10 minutes?
- Does the viewer understand why each step matters?
- Does the video end with something built, not just explained?
- Are definitions short and tied to the demo?
- Are there clear transitions between iPad, facecam, and demo?

## Useful Hook Templates

- “Trong 10 phút tới, mình sẽ thử biến một ý tưởng nhỏ thành một công ty 1 người có thể vận hành bằng AI.”
- “Nếu ngày xưa khởi nghiệp cần một team, thì hôm nay mình thử thay team đó bằng nhân sự số.”
- “Đây không phải video nói AI sẽ làm giàu cho bạn. Đây là video mình thử dựng một luồng kinh doanh nhỏ có thể chạy thật.”
- “Một người, một nguồn hàng, một page, hai nhân sự số. Liệu như vậy đã đủ để bắt đầu chưa?”

## Title Patterns

- “Tôi thử dựng công ty 1 người bằng AI Agents”
- “Khởi nghiệp 1 người với AI: khó hay dễ?”
- “Một người có thể vận hành như một công ty nhờ AI không?”
- “Từ một nguồn hàng đến mini business bằng AI Agents”
- “Không team, ít vốn: dựng hệ thống bán hàng bằng nhân sự số”

## When Research Is Needed

If the user asks for research or current best practices, browse for up-to-date sources. Prefer:
- YouTube official creator resources
- Google/Think with Google resources
- Credible creator economy or video strategy sources

Use research to inform structure, but keep the final plan adapted to the user’s concept and target output.
