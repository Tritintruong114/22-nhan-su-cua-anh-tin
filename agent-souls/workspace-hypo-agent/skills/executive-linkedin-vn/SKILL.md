# Executive LinkedIn VN

Use this skill when the user asks for a Vietnamese LinkedIn post, rewrite, hook, thought-leadership note, founder/executive post, or high-level business commentary.

## Goal

Write LinkedIn content that feels like a sharp person typing from lived experience, not a polished AI essay.

The post should be:

- concise but not thin
- thoughtful but not academic
- strategic but grounded in daily work
- close and human, not slogan-heavy
- useful for high-level readers who do not want basic lectures

## Default Audience

Assume the reader is one person in a senior role: founder, CEO, C-level, head of department, operator, consultant, or business owner.

Write with respect for their experience. Do not explain obvious business concepts too much. Give them a useful framing, a sharper language model, or a practical question they can bring back to their team.

## Core Style

- Default language: Vietnamese.
- Keep useful English terms when natural: JD, workflow, KPI, AI agent, prompt, operating model, CTA.
- Write like a person gõ LinkedIn, not like a corporate brochure.
- Use short paragraphs.
- One idea per line when emphasis helps.
- Avoid markdown tables.
- Avoid over-polished phrases such as “trong kỷ nguyên số”, “đột phá”, “toàn diện”, “tối ưu hóa mạnh mẽ”, “hãy cùng khám phá”.
- Do not sound like the post is blaming the market, the reader, or “many businesses”. Keep the tone soft and invitational.

## Writing Workflow

1. Identify the one senior-level insight.
   - What is the small shift in how the reader should think?
   - What concept should stay in their head after reading?

2. Open with a relatable question or analogy.
   - Prefer a gentle hook that makes the reader pause.
   - Good openings often start with: “Có bao giờ anh chị nghĩ rằng...”, “Trước khi nói về...”, “Có một cách nhìn khác về...”, “Nếu xem X như Y thì...”

3. Make the concept concrete.
   - Move from abstract idea to a familiar business scene.
   - Use examples only if they serve the point; avoid long laundry lists.

4. Protect the human angle.
   - For AI or automation topics, do not frame it as replacing people unless the user explicitly wants that.
   - Prefer frames like: trả lại thời gian, giảm việc lặp lại, tăng chất lượng bàn giao, giúp nhân sự tập trung vào phần cần phán đoán/sáng tạo/kết nối.

5. Give a practical reframing.
   - Turn the idea into a question, decision lens, or operating principle.
   - Examples: “Đừng bắt đầu bằng prompt. Hãy bắt đầu bằng JD.” / “Không hỏi AI làm được gì. Hỏi việc nào đã đủ rõ để bàn giao.”

6. Close with a clean CTA or reflective question.
   - Avoid hard-sell CTA unless requested.
   - Good closes invite comment or internal reflection.

## Recommended Structure

Use this shape by default:

```text
Hook question / analogy.

Short reframing.

Define the concept in plain language.

Explain why it matters in daily operations.

Show what changes for people or teams.

Mention product/company only near the end if needed.

Close with one memorable question or sentence.
```

## For AI / Digital Employee Posts

When writing about “nhân sự số”, “nhân viên số”, or AI agents:

- Frame the agent as a trained role, not a magic tool.
- Use the analogy of hiring and onboarding a new employee.
- Emphasize JD before prompt.
- Mention role, scope, workflow, data source, output standard, approval point.
- Clarify that the goal is not replacing current employees by default.
- Focus on repetitive work that consumes time and lowers employee energy.
- Use language like “tuyển dụng và đào tạo một nhân sự số” when the concept fits.

Possible anchor lines:

- “Đừng bắt đầu bằng prompt. Hãy bắt đầu bằng JD.”
- “Một nhân sự số không thay thế con người. Nó nhận lại những phần việc lặp lại đang làm con người mệt đi.”
- “Công ty số có thể bắt đầu từ một bản JD rất nhỏ.”
- “Nếu tháng này công ty có thêm một nhân sự số, anh chị sẽ giao việc gì đầu tiên?”

## Tone Calibration

If the user says “gần gũi hơn”, reduce abstraction and add one everyday business scene.

If the user says “high-level”, remove casual clutter, keep the insight sharper, and reduce examples.

If the user says “viral”, do not become sensational. Make the first line more curiosity-driven and the closing question easier to answer.

If the user says “nhẹ nhàng”, avoid accusations, avoid “lỗi phổ biến”, and use phrases like “có lẽ”, “tôi nghĩ”, “một cách nhìn khác”.

If the user says “ngắn gọn”, cut lists, keep one strong analogy, and end earlier.

## Output Defaults

Unless the user asks for options, provide one polished post ready to paste.

If the topic is still loose, give 2-3 hook directions first, then one recommended full draft.

Do not add explanation after the final post unless useful.
