## XinChao App UA/GTM research
- Trí assigned me to focus on user acquisition for `https://xinchao.app/`.
- Product: XinChao / `XinChao - Learn Vietnamese`, a mobile app for practical/survival Vietnamese, especially for first-time visitors, expats, and remote workers in Vietnam.
- Public website positioning: `Speak Vietnamese. Survive. Connect.` and `pocket survival kit` for street food, bargaining, culture, everyday Vietnam.
- App Store listing researched 2026-08-30:
  - App: `XinChao - Learn Vietnamese`
  - Subtitle: `Learn Vietnamese Daily`
  - Developer/seller: Tin Truong
  - Category: Education
  - Device: iPhone-only
  - Version history: 1.0 on May 20, 1.0.1 on Jun 17, 1.0.2 on Jun 22
  - Ratings: not enough ratings/reviews displayed yet
  - IAP: Monthly Pro $6.99, Yearly Pro $69.99
  - Claimed capabilities: survival vocabulary/scenarios, food/money/getting around/everyday confidence, speaking feedback, camera-powered tools for signs/menus, dialect options, saved Pocket phrases, goals/streaks.
- Pricing page researched 2026-08-30:
  - 3-day free trial
  - Public yearly price: $69.99/year
  - Listed benefits: Hanoi/Saigon accent toggle, daily life phrases for landlord/Grab/markets/lunch orders, listen-repeat drills, quizzes, survival packs/missions, cloud sync, streaks/XP/badges, seasonal packs.
- Market signals researched:
  - Vietnam tourism remains a large timing opportunity: official tourism data showed July 2026 international visitors at about 1.67M and H1 2026 near 12.3M visitors, up 14.9% YoY, with 25M annual target mentioned by Vietnam tourism reporting.
  - Expat base is smaller than tourism but higher intent/retention: estimates often cite roughly 83.5k-100k foreign nationals/expats in Vietnam, with HCMC/Hanoi/Da Nang as likely concentration points.
  - Language-learning apps are a large market, but generic competitors win breadth/habit; XinChao should not fight Duolingo/Babbel head-on. Win a narrow moment: practical Vietnamese for Vietnam right now.
- Positioning decision:
  - Do not sell `learn Vietnamese` as the main promise.
  - Sell `survive your first 7 days in Vietnam` / `handle real Vietnam moments without freezing`.
  - Core emotional job: reduce anxiety and embarrassment in street situations; create small confidence wins.
- Best initial segments:
  - First-time travelers planning Vietnam trips.
  - Expats/remote workers newly settling in Vietnam.
  - Foreigners with Vietnamese partners/friends/coworkers.
  - Backpackers/street-food travelers who want bargaining/ordering phrases.
- Acquisition strategy:
  - Short-form video first: one real situation per clip, one phrase, one cultural note, one CTA.
  - SEO/blog second: high-intent pages around `how to order food in Vietnamese`, `Vietnamese phrases for tourists`, `Vietnamese numbers for bargaining`, `Hanoi vs Saigon accent`, `Vietnam expat phrases`, `how to say no spicy/no sugar/no ice`.
  - Community distribution: Reddit/Facebook expat/travel groups, but post as helpful mini-guides, not app spam.
  - Partnership: hostels, coworking spaces, relocation/visa newsletters, tour guides, food tour creators, Vietnamese teachers with foreign audiences.
  - Paid UA only after App Store listing screenshots, onboarding, paywall, and analytics are ready.
- ASO checklist:
  - App title/subtitle should include high-intent discovery terms without becoming spammy: `Vietnamese`, `phrases`, `travel`, `speak`, `pronunciation`, `expat`, `Vietnam`.
  - Screenshot 1-3 should sell outcomes: order food, bargain, accents, menu/sign scan, voice practice.
  - Add a short app preview video if possible: street situation -> phrase -> listen/repeat -> confidence.
  - Push early rating loop after user completes a helpful survival pack or saves useful phrases.
  - Test product page variants around traveler vs expat vs food/market use cases.
- Product/UA loop:
  - Content teaches a survival phrase.
  - CTA sends user to a free survival pack.
  - User practices/saves phrases.
  - App prompts a tiny win: use this phrase today / on your trip.
  - After win, ask for rating/share.
  - Pro upsell: accent audio, voice feedback, full packs, camera/menu/sign help.
- Metrics to track:
  - Store page view -> install conversion.
  - Install -> first survival pack complete.
  - First-session time to first useful phrase saved/practiced.
  - Voice practice activation.
  - D1/D7 retention.
  - Trial start and trial-to-paid conversion.
  - Scenario-level conversion: food, Grab, market, landlord, work, dating/social.
  - Rating/review volume.
  - CAC/LTV by channel.
